GOOD QUESTIONS

Know exactly what travels.

PresetBridge makes every compatibility decision visible before export.

Does PresetBridge upload my files?+

No. This front-end prototype processes files and stores its library in your browser’s local storage.

Can it rewrite every `.ffx` preset?+

Not yet. The app generates a compatibility package and report; an Adobe-format parser is required for binary `.ffx` rewriting.

What does safe conversion do?+

It applies documented fallback notes for properties that are commonly unavailable in older targets.
